About GamBasket

The Unified Digital Marketplace of The Gambia

Empowering Gambian merchants, modern shoppers, and gig-economy workers through seamless commerce, verified payments, and door-to-door delivery.

Built for The Gambia

Tailored directly for Gambian currency (Dalasi D), local towns (Banjul, Serrekunda, Brikama, Kololi), and local payment methods (Cash on Delivery, Wave, QMoney).

Multi-Stakeholder Growth

Buyers find quality goods, merchants expand beyond physical storefronts, couriers earn on every delivery run, and operations workers get paid for moderation.

Trust & Buyer Protection

Every merchant is verified and catalog items are moderated to ensure genuine condition, authentic specs, and reliable fulfillment.
