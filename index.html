Questions, answered

Visit the help centre
How do I pay?

Choose cash on delivery at checkout and pay the courier when your order arrives. Other payment methods appear at checkout when a seller offers them.

How does delivery work?

Couriers deliver across Greater Banjul. You get a delivery code; share it with the courier only when you have your item, and the order is marked complete.

What does the verified badge mean?

The seller's identity and business details were checked by the JulaDirect team before the badge was given.

What if something goes wrong?

Open the order in your account and raise a dispute. Our team reviews every case and can arrange a refund.

How do I start selling?

Create an account, open the Seller Centre and add your first product. Listings go live once reviewed.
