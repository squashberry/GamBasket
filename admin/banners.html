Homepage banners

Create the full-screen banner shown on the homepage, switch it on or off, and change it any time.

Only one banner can be live. While it is live, the homepage hides the other banners and the central search bar, and shows a minimal menu bar instead. Turning a banner on turns the current one off.

New banner

Put each headline word or phrase on its own line (up to 3 lines per side).

A page on this site (for example explore.html?sort=newest) or a full https:// link. Leave empty for no button.
A transparent PNG cut-out works best. It is centred between the two headlines.
